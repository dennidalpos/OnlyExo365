## Help-desk sheet

**Exchange Online operations in one desktop interface**

1. Check Worker, Exchange and Graph status separately.
2. Search a mailbox and inspect exposed settings, quotas, licenses and delegates.
3. Search traces by dates and addresses; inspect their events.
4. Review supported fields in security and mail-flow editors.
5. Inspect logs and retain the diagnostic reference on error.

Before saving, verify the target and action summary. After timeout, inspect tenant state before retrying: writes are not automatically retried or rolled back. Use [operator workflows](operator-guide.md), [visual flows](visual-guide.md) and [diagnostics](diagnostics.md).

## Product card

**Exchange Online and Microsoft Graph administration on Windows x64.**

WPF Shell with a separate PowerShell Worker, English/Italian interface, local reports and diagnostics. Available functions include mailbox/recipient management, traces, security/mail-flow settings and exposed Purview actions. Access depends on authentication, tenant permissions and capabilities.

Message Trace allows at most 10 days per search within the last 90. Rule Test evaluates a subset of conditions and may be indeterminate; it is not a delivery simulation. Purview functions have separate prerequisites and do not certify compliance.

The project is proprietary; preserve [LICENSE](../../LICENSE). Add a download call to action only after verifying an authorized distribution channel.
